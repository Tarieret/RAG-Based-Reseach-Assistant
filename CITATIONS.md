## Note on PDF Files

The original PDF files are **not included** in this repository due to copyright restrictions.

### To Use This System:

1. Obtain your own research articles (PDFs) related to your topic of interest
2. Place them in the `documents/` folder
3. Run the database rebuild script: `python rebuild_database.py`
4. The system will process your documents and create a searchable knowledge base

### Recommended Sources for Research Articles:

- **PubMed Central**: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/ (Open access articles)
- **arXiv**: https://arxiv.org/ (Preprints in various fields)
- **Google Scholar**: https://scholar.google.com/
- **Your institution's library**: Access through your university or organization
